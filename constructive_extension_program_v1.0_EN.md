## Main line

The five essays are one line: **nothing "static" comes for free.**

Energy must manifest before it can be weighed (01–02) → things have sizes because dwelling costs rent (03) → stillness comes from confirming (04) → even the reference frame is built from four points (05). This is the constructive-mathematics grammar — numbers cluster, stillness is the steady state of motion, where there is an anchor there is a limit — grown into the physical world.

## Writing principles (series precedent)

- Mainstream facts and system readings are **sidebar-marked throughout** — the reader always knows which sentence is textbook and which is the system's reading;
- Negative readings and open questions are **reported as they are** (no pretending to know);
- Each essay closes with **four verifiable assets** (things the reader can check by hand);
- All numbers are checked against the published records, never from memory.
